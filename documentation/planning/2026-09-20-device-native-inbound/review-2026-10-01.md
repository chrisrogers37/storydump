# Device-native inbound against main — what to keep, how to re-cut, and compression

**Scope.** This reviews the plan in `documentation/planning/2026-09-20-device-native-inbound/` against public `main` at `9be8daf6` (2026-09-30, still the tip on 2026-10-01). The plan merged as `9b8a52d3` on 2026-09-28, and nothing of it is built.
- **Method:** three read-only lanes (platform and execution; media, the mix and publishing; surfaces, docs and roadmap), then an independent re-cut proposal. Every load-bearing claim was re-checked against the code, and the Telegram and Apple facts against their own pages on 2026-10-01.
- **Hashes are new-history.** The plan was last verified at `728b087d`, and 43 commits have landed since.
- **Labels:** C is confirmed by reading; L is likely; H is a hypothesis.

## The short answer

- **Keep the plan's frame.** These still fit main:
  - decision B: a drop's bytes go into the team's own Drive, and Storydump stores nothing;
  - the three rulings of 2026-09-28;
  - the drop's shape: the webhook admits and enqueues, and a worker job makes every provider call.

  Nothing on main brings media in from a phone, so the pain the plan answers still stands.
- **Re-cut the order.**
  - **The drop no longer depends on the 20% share.** Under F12 (b), nothing is written to the mix when a drop folder is created, so phase 03 does not need phase 01. The epic's critical path still routes through 01 (`00_EPIC.md:107`).
  - **The drop can launch dark.** The "Accept drops" switch is off by default, so it can merge switched off and be turned on first in the owner's workspace.
  - **The smallest slice is 02a → 02b → 03a → 03b**, with the drop folder weighted by hand until the reserved share (01) lands on top of #1507.
- **Rewrite two phases; refresh the rest.**
  - **Phase 01:** F12 (b) moved it into `weights()`, and two web cards now share the set of modes.
  - **Phase 03b:** the switch has no writer, the settings contract does not allow the key, the migration checklist moved, and three execution rules are missing.
  - **Phases 00, 02 and 03a:** citations and a few new clauses.
- **One decision is time-sensitive.**
  - **The problem:** Google's verification submission must demo every scope it requests (`documentation/operations/google-oauth-verification.md:31`). Putting `drive.file` in the first submission therefore makes a launch-critical review wait for drops.
  - **The lean:** ask for `drive.file` only when an admin turns drops on.
- **Compression changes the advice, not the plan.** Telegram photos are good enough if people send them in HD. Video is the real constraint, because a bot can download at most 20 MB. The plan's two messages to senders also contradict each other (§5).
- **A live bug outside the plan matters more than any phase.** A file the publish can never fetch stalls its folder: one deleted from Drive, or one over 8 MB (image) or 40 MB (video). A one-folder workspace stops posting until someone taps Skip (§4). Fix it now, at the sync.
- **Wait before running cycle 2.** It should follow the merges of #1505, #1507 and #1517, which touch the files it rewrites, and should wait until a build slot is booked. Migrations 085–089 landed in the two days after the plan merged.

## 1. The system the plan now lands in

- **Posture.**
  - Both services run as restricted logins, so row-level security (per-row tenant filtering) applies to both (`documentation/operations/runtime-database-roles.md:4-11`).
  - Both refuse to boot without a key ring. A ring that cannot load raises `RingUnavailable`, which is not a dead credential (`src/services/target/drive_credentials.py:97-104`).
  - The audit triggers refuse a governance write that carries no actor (`scripts/migrations/085_audit_triggers_empty_actor.sql:66-71`). C.
- **Worker.**
  - **Own transactions.** Provider-facing executors run their own transactions (`src/services/target/work_loop.py:195-202`).
  - **The tripwire.** It raises on a provider call inside a worker session, but only three sites arm it (`unit_of_work.py:333`, `:481`, `:516`); the webhook's raw connection is not one of them.
  - **One tenant setting per block.** A session block applies the tenant and actor once (`unit_of_work.py:460-524`), so anything after a commit inside the block has neither.
  - **The pool is exactly full.** 3 interactive tasks × 1 + 2 bulk tasks × 2 + 3 reserved = 10 (`work_loop.py:70-72`, `:151-158`; `unit_of_work.py:92`).
  - **One workspace can fill a lane.** On a single worker, one workspace can hold a whole lane (`work_loop.py:73-79`).
  - **Spent-budget notices go everywhere.** A spent job's notice goes to every bound group (`work_loop.py:754-777`). C.
- **Ledger and content schedule.**
  - **Built: phases 1–4.** These are `planned` intents (088), and serve-or-miss on time with `fn_prompts_due` re-created (089). A planned story sits outside the daily cap.
  - **In review: phase 5** (#1517: `schedule_item`, `storydump schedule`).
  - **The card is pinned to the door.** Its SELECT is pinned verbatim to the door's body, so a new card column needs a new migration (`tests/src/services/target/test_prompts.py:515-538`). C.
- **The mix.**

- **Publishing.** Every story is framed into 1080×1920. The frame shrinks a large picture (`crop: limit`) and enlarges a small video (`crop: pad`), and is delivered as JPEG or MP4, so HEIC is fine (`src/services/target/transit.py:207-256`, `:573-577`). C. ## 4. Found on the way, and worth doing whatever happens to drops

**1. A file the publish can never fetch stalls its folder.** Confirmed by reading; not reproduced. Not found in public issues on 2026-10-01; tracked in #1430 until it has its own issue.
- **Which files:** one deleted from Drive, or one over 8 MB (image) or 40 MB (video). Phone videos often exceed 40 MB.
- **Why it happens:**
  - **Nothing retires a single file.** The only state writes on `media_items` re-avail rows or retire a removed folder (`media_sync.py:171`, `:206`; `provisioning.py:905`). The design record already notes "the walk has no tombstoning yet" (`03-decision-record.md:207`).
  - **Nothing records size.** The listing has each file's size (`google_drive_adapter.py:247`, `:890`), but the insert drops it (`media_sync.py:667-671`).
  - **The card degrades quietly.** A missing file gets a text-only card (`src/channels/telegram_transport.py:481-487`).
  - **The publish locks nothing.** It fails terminally (`drive_adapter.py:93`, `:98`), refunds, and by a recorded rule locks nothing (`publish_pipeline.py:1757-1759`).
  - **The draw always takes the head.** It picks a folder by weight, then that folder's head, never-posted first (`scheduler.py:385`, `:442-466`).
- **The effect:** every slot for that folder redraws the same file until someone taps Skip, which lasts 45 days. 089's `item_unsupported` miss reason can never fire.
- **The plan named this risk for drops alone** (`00_EPIC.md:89`); the caps cover drops, not files added by hand.
- **Fix it at the sync,** where Drive's state enters. The publish's no-lock rule is deliberate.
  - Land an oversized file as `unsupported` from the listing's size.
  - Tombstone files that a complete walk no longer lists.
  - Name the file in the notice.

## 5. Compression with Telegram as transport

**What Telegram does.** Checked on Telegram's pages, 2026-10-01; current Bot API 10.3.
- **Bot downloads stop at 20 MB.** "For the moment, bots can download files of up to 20MB in size." A self-hosted Bot API server removes the download limit.
- **A photo sent as a photo is re-encoded.** Standard is the default, about 1280 px on the long edge. HD (since 2025-06-03, "4 times the pixels", about 2560 px) is chosen per send: "tap the SD button … or 'Send In High Quality'". A file is untouched.
- **The bot sees what arrived.** It gets each photo as a list of sizes with width and height. It gets each video with width, height and `qualities`: alternative encodings, each with its own file id, size and codec.
- **Sharing from another app can lose quality.** That path, the phone's share sheet, has had bugs where HD and send-as-file were ignored (Android, 2025; one marked fixed, one reopened). L: still possible on some clients.

**What it costs once the story is built.** Read from `transit.py:207-256`; the softness is L.

| How it was sent | What arrives | In the 1080×1920 story |
|---|---|---|
| Photo, standard | ~1280 px long edge | Landscape is fine. Portrait (~960×1280) falls short of the frame: likely kept at its size over a blurred copy, not enlarged (L) |
| Photo, HD | ~2560 px | Full story quality |
| Photo as a file | the original | Full quality. Above 8 MB it is refused; a 48 MP JPEG can exceed that (L), while HEIC usually does not |
| Video, compressed by the client | the client's chosen quality (L) | At 720p it is enlarged 1.5×, visibly soft; at 480p, 2.25× |
| Video as a file or original | often above 20 MB | The bot cannot download it; the plan refuses it with a reply |

**The other costs:**
- **Drive is the team's archive too.** A compressed drop archives the compressed copy, which was the owner's first worry.
- **Duplicates.** Dedup is by exact bytes, so a compressed drop and the original already in a folder are two items, and both can post (L).

**Where the plan falls short:**
- **The two messages contradict each other.** The daily hint says "send as a file for full quality" (spec `:141-143`; decision `:154-155`). A file over 8 MB is refused with "send it as a photo, not as a file" (`03_the-drop.md:70`; checklist `:99`).
- **The hint fires on every compressed photo,** HD ones included, which are already at story quality.
- **A video over 20 MB is refused** even when a smaller encoding in `qualities` would fit.
- **Nothing records what quality actually arrived.**

**What to change.** These go to cycle 2; F17 asks the owner to approve the new wording.
1. **Recommend HD, not files, in both messages.** HD fits every cap and reaches full story quality. A file is for archive-quality originals under 8 MB.
2. **Hint only when it matters:** when the largest photo size is under 1920 px on the long edge.
3. **Fetch the best video that fits.** Download the largest encoding in `qualities` under 20 MB before refusing; refuse only when none fits.
4. **Record what arrived.** Put `sent_as`, width and height on the job and the Drive file, so the four-week read can report how much arrived compressed and how often a story was enlarged.
5. **Keep two levers in reserve.**
   - A self-hosted Bot API server would let video originals through, up to the 40 MB publish limit. It is one more always-on service.
   - The 8 MB image limit applies to the source file, although the frame shrinks it to 1080×1920 anyway. Raising it is a separate change that would help Drive folders too.

   Neither belongs in the first slice.
6. **Say it in the guide.** Drops trade some quality for convenience; for originals, upload through the Drive app (item 6 in §4).

**Verdict.** Compression does not overturn the drop.
- **Photos:** HD solves the problem, and the advice should say so.
- **Videos:** the 20 MB ceiling is the binding constraint. `qualities` softens it. Measure before running another service.
