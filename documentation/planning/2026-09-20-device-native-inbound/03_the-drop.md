## Summary

The dispatcher recognises a photo or video from a linked, active member in a bound group and enqueues one job, inside a savepoint in the delivery's own transaction, so nothing it does can fail or redeliver the delivery. The job runs on the interactive lane, serialized per workspace, and owns its transactions: it checks the workspace's "Telegram drops" folder under the current Drive grant or creates one (writing its `role`; under F12 (b) nothing is written to the mix), downloads the file from Telegram, uploads it into the folder, records the item at once and mints a demand sync as a backstop, and reacts to the message. A refusal is one reply, paced and threaded to the message, written with the job's own finalize so it is sent exactly once; a drop that runs out of retries is told so. Nothing is accepted that the story path cannot carry, and GIFs, stickers and video notes are not drops. Two PRs: **03a** adds the Telegram transport methods and the Drive write leg, pure additions with their tests; **03b** adds the migration, the recognition, the admission, the executor, the surfaces and the docs. F11 ruled (b): an admin switch per bound group, off by default, gates every drop, and any linked, active member may drop while it is on.

Whenever it refuses, it writes the reply in the same transaction as `jobs.finalize_job` and returns `jobs.SELF_FINALIZED`, so a refusal is sent exactly once; it logs its outcome and a refusal's reason, and the reply's payload carries a machine-readable `reason`.

In a session: read the workspace's live drop source (`provider='gdrive' AND config->>'role'='telegram_drops' AND NOT removed`, with `workspace_id = :ws`), the grant's scopes (`drive_credentials.grant_scopes`) and the payload; close the block. A grant that is not active or not writable is refused. This is ordinary, not a rare race — a Disconnect or a lapsed grant leaves the switch on — so the refusal is paced under `drop_notice`, per reason and per media group, and a Disconnect turns every binding's switch off (step 8).
   2. With a source: `folder_writable` under the current grant. When the grant cannot see the folder or cannot write it — a reconnect under another Google account, a trashed folder — retire the role and pause the old row as Remove does, so it does not stand on the attention panel as a folder that stopped syncing, and continue as if there were no source.
