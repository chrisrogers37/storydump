### Fixed

- **A sync whose retries ran out could leave its Drive folder never syncing again, if the notice about it could not be written (#1443).** When a sync job spends its retry budget, the worker re-arms the source for tomorrow and tells the workspace. The re-arm ran inside the notice's savepoint, so a notice that could not be written rolled the re-arm back with it: the source stayed active with no next sync, no job and no alert, and nothing brought it back. The re-arm now commits in the job's own finalize transaction, ahead of the notice.
