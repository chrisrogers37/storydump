### Changed

- **A story parked for its Instagram past-posts content says why, in plain words.** Its notice and its review card now name the refusal its evidence records: Instagram's HTTP status, error code, subcode, type and message, or the check its file failed and the value it read, such as a codec stories do not take, a duration outside 3 to 60 seconds or a file over the byte limit. A record with nothing specific is still said: its error's class, its kind, or that no reason was recorded, never a cause the record cannot support. The card's line reads the story's `last_error -> 'error' -> 'refusal'`, so the card, the settled-card sweep and the sender's edit all say the same.
