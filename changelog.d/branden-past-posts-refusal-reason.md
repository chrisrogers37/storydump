### Changed

- **A story parked for its Instagram past-posts content says why, in plain words.** Its notice and its review card now name the refusal its evidence records: Instagram's HTTP status, error code, subcode, type and message, or the check its file failed and the value it read, such as an edit list, a duration outside 3 to 60 seconds or a file over the byte limit. A park whose evidence says nothing more keeps the old notice. The card's line reads the story's `last_error -> 'error' -> 'refusal'`, so the card, the settled-card sweep and the sender's edit all say the same.
