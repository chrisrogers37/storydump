### Fixed

- **The index no longer retires a slide a story is waiting to post on one read that lacks it.** When a complete Instagram read of a carousel lacks a slide that a live story holds (one neither finished nor asked to cancel), the index keeps the slide after the read's own, marks the stored list incomplete and leaves its count at what the read listed. That story's own fetch then decides: two fresh reads prove the slide gone and retire it with the evidence, or the slide is downloaded and posts. A kept slide is judged against the read's count plus itself, and a listed slide against the count of the read that listed it, so no slide's state moves another's proof. Slides no story holds retire as before. So does a held slide when the read already lists a full 20-slide carousel, or on a source that takes only each post's first slide, where a kept slide would no longer be first. A refused slide's evidence now also records the count it was judged against.
