### Fixed

- **The sample workspace's times are current for every visitor (#1649).** `/demo` was prerendered and rebuilt hourly, and the visitor whose request set off a rebuild got the old copy: one Queue served at 3:51 PM showed its scheduled posts at 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM that day. The visitor's browser now builds the sample when the page opens, from its own clock, so no copy made ahead of the visit can be shown; the page stays prerendered and draws a placeholder until then.
- **The sample workspace offers what a new workspace's Queue offers (#1649).** Direct posting is off by default, so a story waiting for a tap offers Posted myself, Skip and Reject, read from the Queue's own `actionsFor`; Approve and its "this would post right away" note are gone. Posted myself records a post made by hand, and nothing in the sample posts.
- **Every screen of the sample workspace tells one story (#1649).** The Overview's counts, chart and mix are counted from the same finished stories that Recent activity and the Calendar show. The chart once drew five posts on a day whose every slot was still in the Queue. The Overview now follows the visitor's taps, as a real workspace's counts would move, and the Calendar draws each story once, as the real Calendar's lanes do: a story waiting for a tap is In Queue under its name, a scheduled one is Predicted, and one marked Posted myself is Posted, in its slot's day.
- **The sample's end panel waits for the visitor's taps (#1649).** Opening the three pages was enough to bring up "That's the job: one tap per Story." with nothing decided; it now appears after three decisions.
- **The Overview's all-clear no longer contradicts a Queue of waiting posts (#1649).** It said "no post is waiting on a decision" beside posts awaiting approval. It and the review condition now use the Queue's own word for a post parked for review: "no post needs attention", "2 posts need attention". The dashboard shows the same sentences.
- **The navigation drawer closes when a link in it is followed (#1649).** The dashboard's and the sample's drawers stayed open over the page they had just opened. Both now draw one `NavDrawer`, which closes on the tap that follows a link, and which gives the dashboard's drawer its title for screen readers.
- **The sample workspace keeps the landing's clock and spelling (#1649).** It runs on London time, as every card on the landing does, and says "Times are in London time." rather than a zone id; the landing's Queue mock shows the bare handle, as the real Queue does.
