### Security

- **The member-removal door checks its caller, a removal spends the person's pending links for the workspace, and the removal record is the membership doors' alone (migration 102, `07` §45).** `fn_member_remove` refuses a workspace that is not the caller's claimed tenant, or a remover who is not an owner or admin of it, with a raise known by its message. `remove_member` also retires the removed person's live bind and connect states for that workspace (the Telegram group bind, the Google Drive and Instagram connects) in the same transaction. The runtime logins no longer read `workspace_member_removals`: their grant and its tenant read policy are gone, and only the membership doors read or write it. Removals made before 090 are recorded from the governance audit trail: each person's latest removal, when another person made it and they are not a member again; a record the door already wrote is kept. The pending invitations those removals outrank are then revoked by the same statement as 098's one-time revoke: one sent before the removal to the removed person's email or Telegram id, and one sent by a removed inviter who is not a member again. `fn_group_member_seen` names `pg_temp` last on its `search_path`. The Members card says that the group does not add a removed person back unless they are invited again. A schema change, awaiting the owner's approval.
