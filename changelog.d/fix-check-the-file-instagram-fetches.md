### Added

- **The file Instagram fetches for a story is checked against Instagram's story spec before Instagram is asked to fetch it.** Once the transit store's story frame serves, its size from the probe's range answer is held to Instagram's limit (100 MB for a video, 8 MB for an image). A video's head is read up to the end of its `moov` (at most 512 KiB, in one more ranged read) for where `moov` sits, its edit lists, its sample entries and each audio track's codec, rate and channel count, the last two from the AAC configuration rather than the entry's own fields. A frame over the size limit, or a video whose audio is not AAC or runs above 48 kHz, parks the story for review at once with no container call, with the check and what it measured on its card and the reason in the workspace's notice. A `moov` after the media, an edit list and more than 2 audio channels are recorded and refuse only with their switches, since Instagram's answer to the container call judges an output nobody has measured yet. Everything read is recorded with the probe's observation on the container permit, along with whatever was left unread and why. The source walker and the derivation's reader share one box reader.
