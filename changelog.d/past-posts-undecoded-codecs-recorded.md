### Changed

- **A past post's video is no longer refused for a codec the probe does not decode.** The story derivation re-encodes every stream, so a sample entry outside H.264, HEVC and AAC (an MPEG-4 Part 2 video, a timecode or text track) is recorded in the evidence as `undecoded_codecs`, and the file is judged on what the probe reads of its container. The probe still opens only those three decoders, and ffmpeg's line for a decoder it keeps closed is not taken as a fault of the file. ffprobe ends when it cannot open a stream's decoder, as for an MPEG-4 Part 2 video, MP3 audio in an `mp4a` entry or a cover picture, so a probe stopped only by such a decoder gives no facts: the file is judged by its derivation, as one with more streams than the probe opens, and its evidence says so (`probe_skipped`, whose `closed_decoders` names the decoders the probe declined, as ffprobe names them). A cover picture is a stream to the probe and no track to the container walk, so one that takes a file past the probe's own stream bound no longer leaves it pending: the file is judged by its derivation the same way, and its evidence records the bound and the tracks the walk counted. A video already refused for its codec stays refused until its asset is observed again, and its card keeps its sentence (`refusal_evidence.LEGACY_CHECKS`).
