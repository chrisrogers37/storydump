### Fixed

- **A past post whose fetch is refused without proof is tried again instead of parking at once.** A refusal that proves nothing (a CDN that refused the link, a Graph error that is not Graph's own "does not exist", an owner the turn could not prove, a download cut off by its time budget) no longer marks the slide unavailable. It records `download_pending` on the slide with the refusal, the attempt and the time of the next try, and the story waits: before that time it is deferred with no Graph request and no download. The wait doubles from 30 s to a 15 min cap, and the eighth refusal parks the story with the last one's evidence, so every try starts inside the hour a story may post late. A refusal that is the same on every try parks at once: Graph saying the post does not exist (code 100, subcode 33), a file over the byte cap, and a slide whose kind Instagram names but whose link it withholds on two reads in a row. On the index side, one parent answer that does not prove the post gone retires none of its slides; the parent is read again at the next restart. A missing suitability verdict now waits for revalidation rather than parking the story as unavailable. The attempt ceiling, the wait's base and cap, and each kind's download time budget (2 min for an image, 4 min for a video) are `WorkerConfig` numbers, and a download its budget cuts off names the budget and the host in its evidence; a story parked on it says "Instagram's file did not download within the 240 s limit" in its reason line.
