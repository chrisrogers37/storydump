### Added

- **A past-posts story's file is fetched by a job of its own, on a new file lane.** The prompt sweep no longer downloads a file: it mints one `prepare_past_post` job per story whose file is due, keyed by the story, on the `media` lane, so a slow download holds one media task and never the cards behind it. The worker runs one media task beside two interactive and two bulk (`TARGET_WORKER_MEDIA_CONCURRENCY`); the interactive lane goes from three tasks to two so that the pool of ten connections still fits, and a workspace holds at most one file task at a time. A job whose faults spend its budget parks its story for review and says so, as a dead publish job's story is. Migration `006` adds the job kind and the lane.

### Changed

- **A past-posts cadence story may post up to an hour after its slot, and one whose file never came is parked for review instead of expiring silently.** The reaper no longer expires a past-posts cadence story of an active, unpaused workspace at its slot: the story may post up to `planned_late_seconds` after it (an hour, the window a planned story has). A download that could no longer finish inside that hour is not started, and a story still waiting when the hour is up, with no try running, is parked for review by the reaper and told in its chats. Either way its reason line says its file could not be fetched within 60 minutes of its slot, how many tries it had, and what refused the last. A paused workspace's story still expires at its slot. Migration `006` gives the reaper door a fifth argument that is false unless a worker passes it, so a worker that does not, an older one included, expires the story at its slot as before. It adds the door that lists the late stories (`fn_reaper_late_past_posts`), and keeps a story whose job is ready or running out of the preparation door.

### Fixed

- **A past post whose fetch is refused without proof is tried again instead of parking at once.** A refusal that proves nothing (a CDN that refused the link, a Graph error that is not Graph's own "does not exist", an owner the turn could not prove, a download cut off by its time budget) no longer marks the slide unavailable. It records `download_pending` on the slide with the refusal and the attempt, and the story's job runs again after a wait that doubles from 30 s to a 15 min cap; a wait that asks Instagram nothing gives its try back. The eighth refused try parks the story with the last one's evidence, so every try starts inside the hour a story may post late. A refusal that is the same on every try parks at once: Graph saying the post does not exist (code 100, subcode 33), a file over the byte cap, and a slide whose kind Instagram names but whose link it withholds on two reads in a row. On the index side, one parent answer that does not prove the post gone retires none of its slides; the parent is read again at the next restart. A missing suitability verdict now waits for revalidation rather than parking the story as unavailable. The attempt ceiling, the wait's base and cap, and each kind's download time budget (2 min for an image, 4 min for a video) are `WorkerConfig` numbers, and a download its budget cuts off names the budget and the host in its evidence; a story parked on it says "Instagram's file did not download within the 240 s limit" in its reason line. Each refused try logs one line naming the story, what refused it and which try it was, and the worker refuses to start with a retry ladder whose tries cannot all start inside the late window.
- **A publish job that meets a slide a refused fetch parked parks the story at once, saying what refused it.** It used to wait as for a policy refusal and park only once its waits were spent, with no reason. The story's `last_error` and its review notice now carry the refusal that parked the slide.
