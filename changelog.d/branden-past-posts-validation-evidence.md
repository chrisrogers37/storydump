### Added

- **A past post that fails local validation keeps the check and what it measured.** The refusal recorded for a story (the asset's `validation_capture -> 'refusal'`, the story's `last_error -> 'error' -> 'refusal'` and the park's audit detail) now names the check that failed and the values it read. For a video that means duration, frame rate and frame count; width, height, sample aspect ratio and aspect; the video and audio codecs, sample rate and channels; or the pixel format. For a container refusal it is the box list, the box count and the sample codecs, for an image its format, and for an oversized file its byte count and the cap. Only parsed facts are kept, never a tool's output, and a validation's fingerprint is unchanged.
