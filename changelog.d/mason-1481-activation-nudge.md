### Added

- **The activation nudge is built, and switched off (#1481).** A daily worker sweep can send one email, once, to a person who stopped part-way through setup: at connecting Instagram, adding a Drive folder, or approving a first story, the last only once a card was offered, so a folder with nothing to approve is never blamed on the person. The email names the step and links straight to it. Migration 106 adds `users.activation_nudge_at`, the once-per-person latch the sweep sets as it queues the email; makes `activation_nudge_sweep` a system job kind in both job-kind constraints; moves the funnel's stage definition into `fn_activation_stages`, which 104's `fn_activation_funnel` now reads with the same result; and adds `fn_activation_stalled`, the door listing who is owed a nudge, executable by `svc_worker` alone. Nothing sends today: the sweep is parked unless `TARGET_ACTIVATION_NUDGE_ENABLED` is set, an email provider is configured and the web origin is known, and turning it on waits on email being enabled and on the owner approving the copy.
