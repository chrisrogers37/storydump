### Added

- **The Overview shows each account's days of content left, and the workspace is told once when an account drops below a week (#1478).** Days left is the files the planner could pick for the account now, over its posts per day (its own, else the workspace's), in whole days rounded down, counted by the planner's own rule: `category_mix.pool` is the read the draw itself makes, so the figure and the draw cannot disagree. It is a floor, because a posted file that comes back off its `recent` lock is not counted ahead of time, and an account the clock does not post for (inactive, or its workspace paused) shows "Not posting" rather than a number. The notice goes through the empty library's own path, the workspace's push bindings, when a mint leaves fewer than 7 days, once per crossing: it re-arms only when the account climbs back to 8 days, so a file coming off its repost lock cannot buy a second notice. The latch is a pair of `audit_events` rows on the account (`low_content_notice`, `low_content_rearmed`), so no migration. The notice never decides the slot: a slot that mints finalizes `succeeded` whether or not anyone was told. With no push binding the latch row records that nobody was (`told: 0`) and a warning is logged once per crossing, the Overview's card being that workspace's channel; the notice is written in a savepoint, so a failure writing it is logged and the mint stands. Only the empty library's notice, where nothing was minted, still parks the job `review_required`. The new read is `GET /api/v1/workspaces/{ws}/runway`.
