### Fixed

- **A slide whose read cannot prove it left is tried again after a wait, then parked with its evidence, instead of being read again on every sweep.** When Instagram's children read of a carousel is truncated and lacks the selected slide (`slide_changed`), the slide's capture records `download_pending` with that evidence, the attempt and the time of the next try. Until then the story is deferred straight after its row read, with no source read, Graph request or download. The wait doubles from 30 s to a 15 min cap, and the eighth such read parks the story with its `slide_changed` evidence, so a slide that never reaches proof costs at most 8 fetches over about 45 minutes rather than a full read every few seconds for as long as the story waits. The attempt ceiling and the wait's base and cap are `WorkerConfig` numbers. Every other refusal is handled as before.
