### Added

- **A visitor who has not signed in can try Storydump on a sample workspace at `/demo`, with nothing to connect (#1480).** The dashboard's own Overview, Queue and Calendar, drawn by its own components and derivations over a made-up workspace (Example Co, one account, three folders). In the Queue the visitor can Approve, Skip or Reject the stories waiting on a decision; each decided row keeps its place and says what the tap would have done in a real workspace, the Calendar follows the decisions, and a closing panel leads to the waitlist, with Sign in second. Nothing leaves the browser: no request, no storage and no cookie, which `demo-isolation-contract.test.ts` pins. The pages are prerendered, rebuilt hourly so the sample's times stay current, and not indexed. The Queue's rows moved into `QueueView`, which the real Queue shares: its `QueueList` keeps the one API call, pinned for all seven actions, and the sidebar takes its entries from its caller.
