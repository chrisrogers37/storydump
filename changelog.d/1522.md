### Fixed

- **The reconciler asks Instagram about an ambiguous publish with no transaction open (#1508).** The egress floor refuses a provider call made inside a transaction, and `reconcile_ambiguous` polled the container's status inside one: the job's before #1492, the row's after it. So in production every ladder row failed with `TransactionDisciplineError`. Its ladder never moved, so an ambiguous publish with a container could neither resolve nor park for review, and its account's next publish waited behind it (`uq_publish_exclusive`). This was latent: no intent was ambiguous. A ladder row now asks the provider before its transaction opens (`reconciler.observe`), then claims its workspace, reads how far its ladder has climbed and records the answer in that one transaction. `reconcile_intent` takes the observed status, and the stories listing, instead of calling a provider. `tests/scripts/test_l3_permit_rail.py` drives the real Graph adapter through the floor, with only the network, DNS and the token faked.
