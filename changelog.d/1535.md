### Added

- **A person can schedule a story from the Media Library (#1413, phase 6a).** Each item has a Schedule… action: pick an account a story can be planned onto (active, or needing reconnection; the picker starts on an active one) and a date and time, and the story is sent for approval at that time like any other; nothing posts until someone approves it. The time is the account's own: the dialog names the zone it is read in (the account's, else the workspace's, else UTC, as `schedule_item` reads it) and converts nothing in the browser. When a lock can be overridden (the item was skipped, or posted on that account, recently) the dialog says so and asks before scheduling it anyway; anything else that keeps an item out is named and final. A refused time says which rule it broke (already passed, skipped by a daylight-saving change, more than 365 days ahead, not a date and a time), a duplicate says whether its story is already planned or a regular slot, or was cancelled a moment ago and can be planned again once it clears, and a missing account or item says which. After scheduling, the dialog says when approval is asked, in that zone, and warns when no Telegram chat is linked, since nothing is asked until one is. The override question and the result take focus as they appear, each read with its sentence, and closing the dialog returns focus to the item. The web reads accounts' `tz` (`Destination`), offers `schedule_item` on the command route, and pins `no_push_binding`, the plan horizon, the lock kinds, the refused-time rules and the account states a story can be planned onto to the API's vocabulary. The Queue badge, filter and Reschedule and Cancel, and the calendar's planned rows, are phase 6b.
