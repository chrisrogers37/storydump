### Changed

- **The typed destination route takes a handle; a `provider_account_ref` sent to it is refused.** `POST /api/v1/workspaces/{ws}/accounts` takes `{"handle": …}` and stores the provisional `manual:<handle>` reference, as before. A body carrying `provider_account_ref` is refused with 400 `account_ref_requires_connect`, with or without a handle beside it, and nothing is written. A destination's real account id is written only by connecting the account (`POST …/accounts/connect` and the Instagram callback); the connect path is unchanged.

- **A workspace's TTL settings are bounded: at least one unit, at most a year.** `settings_change` refuses `repost_ttl_days` or `skip_ttl_days` outside 1–365 and `approval_ttl_minutes` outside 1–525,600 as `invalid_args`, naming the setting and its range, before anything is written (`workspaces.SETTINGS_RANGES`, from the bounds in `vocabulary`). The three columns carry no CHECK; a year is the range the web's lock fields already offer. NULL still means the deployment's default, and every other setting is validated as before.
