### Fixed

- **A day in the sample workspace's Calendar now opens, so "+N more" leads somewhere.** A day drew three chips and "+N more", and no day could be opened, so a story the Queue named could be out of reach on the Calendar: at 3 PM London time two of the three scheduled stories sat under today's "+3 more". A day is now a link to the product's own day view over the sample's stories, every state in slot order. The sample still draws one month: `ContentCalendar` takes `monthLinks` for that, and `CalendarDay` draws without a workspace.
- **A click or a tap on the posting chart no longer leaves an outline box around its bars.** The chart library draws its bars inside a focusable layer, and a browser outlines a focused SVG element. Those layers now take no outline. Keyboard focus still rings the whole chart. The dashboard's Overview and the sample workspace share the chart, so both are fixed.
- **A Queue row on a phone no longer breaks its date across lines.** A scheduled row's line was squeezed beside its badge and read "Sat, Oct / 10, 5:00 PM". The date and time are now one piece that cannot break, and the row's text keeps enough width that the badge wraps below it on a narrow screen.
- **The landing page's mocks no longer say "Today" beside a fixed date.** The hero's chat is headed by its card's own day, taken from the slot, and the Queue mock under "Where the tap happens" has no "Today" label.
- **The Posting mix's columns add up.** Each share was rounded on its own, so a column could read 101%. Each column is now rounded as a whole, on the Overview and on the sample workspace's.
- **The Calendar's day view shows a story's whole name on a phone.** Beside the picture, the time and a state such as "awaiting approval", a 390 px screen left a name 22 px, so it read "m…". The row now wraps there: the state drops below the name, and a long name takes a second line instead of being cut. From 640 px up the row is one line, as before. This is the product's day view, which the sample workspace now opens too.
