### Changed

- **A story video is encoded to Instagram's story spec, and a video longer than a story is trimmed to 60 seconds instead of refused.** The transit derivation, which is the file Instagram fetches for every story video, now pins H.264 high profile at level 4.2, AAC audio at 48 kHz, a frame rate of 23 to 60 fps, a variable bit rate capped at 13 Mbit/s and a 60 second trim. The rules come from the validation matrix, where each sits beside the line of Instagram's story specification it meets. The bit-rate cap is the lower of Instagram's 25 Mbit/s maximum and its 100 MB file size at 60 seconds. A past-posts video over 60 seconds is now supported: its card says "✂️ Trimmed to 60 s", and its evidence records that it is trimmed and the encoding its derivation is sent. A video under 3 seconds is still refused. The matrix and evidence versions move, so each stored supported verdict is judged again once.
