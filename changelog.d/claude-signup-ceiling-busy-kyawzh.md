### Changed

- **A full waitlist ceiling is its own refusal, says "busy" on the site, and alerts the operators.** When the 600 accepted signups a minute that all visitors share (#1582) are spent, `POST /public/waitlist` answers 429 with `reason: full` instead of the bare 429 a visitor's own limit gives, and the landing form shows "Lots of people are joining right now. Please try again in a minute." for it and for the slot refusal `busy`, instead of "Something went wrong", with the form's error event recorded as `busy`. Each API process logs `waitlist: Waitlist signups reached the ceiling …` and sends it to each operator who has linked Telegram at most once every 10 minutes, counting the refusals in between; neither names an address, a visitor or a campaign. A new test pins that at the ceiling an address already on the list gets the same answer as a new one, so the 429 is no oracle for who has joined.
