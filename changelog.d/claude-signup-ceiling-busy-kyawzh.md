### Changed

- **A full waitlist ceiling (#1582) is its own refusal, says "busy" on the site, and alerts the operators.** When a limit all visitors share is spent, the 600 accepted signups a minute or, with `WAITLIST_SITE_SECRET` matched, the 300 a minute shared by calls with no usable visitor address, `POST /public/waitlist` answers 429 with `reason: full` instead of the bare 429 a visitor's own limit gives. The landing form shows "Lots of people are joining right now. Please try again in a minute." for it and for the slot refusal `busy`, instead of "Something went wrong". Each API process logs `waitlist: Waitlist … hit their shared limit of …` and sends it to each operator who has linked Telegram at most once every 10 minutes for each limit, counting the refusals in between; the alert waits its turn behind queued signup messages but is never dropped for a full queue, and neither names an address, a visitor or a campaign. A new test pins that at the ceiling an address already on the list gets the same answer as a new one, so the 429 is no oracle for who has joined.
