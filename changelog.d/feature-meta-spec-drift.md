### Added

- **When Instagram refuses a story file that passed storydump's checks, the refusal is recorded beside what the checks read, and the operators are told.** A refusal that ends the story counts: a container Instagram reports `ERROR`, or any container or publish error that parks or fails it, except a dead token and throttling. Its `spec_drift` record (the frame's size, its container and audio facts, its encoding and the limits it was held to, beside Instagram's code, subcode, message and trace id) goes on the permit, or beside a dead container's reason on the story. After the run, each person in `OPS_USER_IDS` who has linked Telegram gets a direct message from the worker's bot with Instagram's answer (an object id in its words shown as `<id>`, as on a review card), at most once a day per Instagram answer, latched in `rate_counters` under a new `ops_notice` scope (migration `005_ops_notice_rate_scope.sql`). The worker needs `OPS_USER_IDS` for this; without it, or without the bot, it logs `ops notice: off` at startup and each refusal on a `publish_pipeline` line. Every story video's container permit now also records the encoding its frame was derived with, from Drive or from past posts.
