### Added

- **The outbox records why a delivery failed, and a monitor can alert when deliveries fail (migration 101, `07` §44; #1482).** `channel_outbox` gains `last_failure_class` (one of `rate_limited`, `destination_gone`, `refused`, `credential_dead` or `ambiguous`), `last_error_code` (the provider's own code, NULL when no answer came back) and `last_failed_at`. `settle` writes them in the same CAS update as the state change, so a writer that loses the row records nothing. A dead token's 401 is now recorded as `credential_dead` instead of as a lost response. Its state is unchanged; #1493 decides whether it should fail at once. `GET /health/delivery` counts the last hour's failures by class and code, with the failed-or-ambiguous total and the rows sent in the same hour, through two `svc_maintenance` doors. `scripts/delivery_monitor.py` alerts at 5 or more failures an hour, clears only after two polls at 1 or fewer, and repeats every 6 hours. It is not installed yet; `documentation/operations/delivery-monitor.md` carries its units.
