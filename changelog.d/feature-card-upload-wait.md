### Fixed

- **A chat card's photo or video can no longer appear two to four times when Telegram's answer to its upload is slow or lost.** A card used to be one upload, and an answer later than the wait read as lost, so the outbox sent the card again, up to three more times, each copy without its buttons. A card is now sent as its text, a small message answered at once, and its photo or video is put onto that same message by the edit that brings its buttons, so a slow or late answer can never leave a second card. That upload is made once, under its own `card_upload` wait (2 minutes): when its answer is lost, when Telegram refuses the file, or when the file cannot be fetched, the text card stands and still gets its line and its buttons, with no second upload; a flood wait holds the chat and the same upload is made again after it. If a lost upload lands later after all, the next edit that writes a line finds the picture, writes the line as its caption, and the card's record follows. The chat sees the card's text a moment before its picture. The 60 s wait of the other uploads and downloads (the transit store, Drive, Instagram) is unchanged.
- **A story whose card could not be confirmed in a chat is no longer silent.** When every send of a card loses its answer, the card ends `failed` and the worker logs a warning (the row, the chat binding, the sends and the last failure's class and code); when the chat holds no other card of the story, it is told once that the story's card could not be confirmed and that the story waits in the Queue on the web. Before, the story waited with no card that had buttons, and nothing said so.
- **Telegram's flood answer sent as a 400 ("Too Many Requests: retry after N") is paced instead of refused.** It was read as a definitive refusal, which lost a card's media, failed a notice or a card's edit outright, and made further calls into the flood. It now writes the wait Telegram named as a pacing hold, as a 429 does. Any other 400 is still a refusal.

### Added

- **A failed attempt of a call that creates something now logs how long it waited and how it failed.** For a card's upload, a Telegram message or any other call the egress floor never sends again once it may have reached the provider, the worker logs each attempt that fails at the transport: the provider's host, the seconds it waited and the transport's exception (a read or write timeout, a connection that failed), and whether the request may have reached the provider. A slow answer can then be told from a lost one. The path is never logged.
- **Each lost answer and each give-up in the outbox logs a warning.** It names the row, its kind, the attempt and the failure's class and code, and, for a card whose upload lost its answer, that its picture is given up, so a card or an edit sent again after a lost answer, and a row that ran out of resends, are visible in the worker log.
