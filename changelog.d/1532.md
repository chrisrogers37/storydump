### Fixed

- **A dead Telegram bot token no longer leaves its outbox rows ambiguous, to be resent until their budget runs out (#1493).** A 401 is the credential's answer, not a lost one: every resend meets it again until the token is replaced. `settle` now treats `CredentialDead` (the transport's `TelegramAuthDead`) as definitive, like a refused message: the row fails after its one attempt, recorded as `credential_dead` with its 401 (101), and nothing resends it. The binding stays, because the chat is not gone. The transport is still the loud surface: it logs the dead token once and counts `auth_failures`. Whether a dead credential should also pause sending bot-wide is the owner's open question.
