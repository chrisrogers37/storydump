### Added

- **An admin invites a person from the Members card, and the join link is shown once (#1563, #1564).** Settings › General › Members has an invite form for admins and owners: an email address and a role, Member or Admin. A successful invitation shows its join link once, with a copy control and "Send this link to them. It works once.", and the link is kept nowhere else: no storage, no analytics, no log, no URL. The invitations not yet accepted are listed beneath, with the date each expires on the workspace's clock. The link is the API's `join_url`; where that is null, the card says the link can't be built. A member's page shows none of this and does not read the invitations.
