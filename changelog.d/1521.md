### Fixed

- **The dashboard reads its dates on the workspace's clock, not the server's or the browser's (#1511).** Three dashboard pieces read dates in whatever zone the code ran in. Recent Activity formatted each row's time on that clock, so outside UTC the server's HTML and the browser's render disagreed and React threw a hydration error (React error 418) on every Overview load. The posting chart labelled every bar one day early west of Greenwich: `posts_by_day.local_date` is the workspace's own day, and read through `Date` it became UTC midnight, shown on the viewer's clock as the day before. The media calendar took today from the UTC date and placed every post, queued story and predicted slot by the date part of its UTC timestamp, so from the evening in New York it highlighted tomorrow and drew an evening slot on the next day. Recent Activity and the calendar now read every time and day in the workspace's zone, the one the Queue's times and the calendar page's "Posts Today" already use: the Overview reads the workspace's config beside its other reads (a failed read is its unavailable state, as for every other dependency), and the calendar's page passes the zone it already read. The chart labels each bar with the day its row names. Both components take the zone as an optional `tz` prop: without one, Recent Activity reads the clock it runs on, as before, and the calendar reads UTC. `landing/src/lib/zoned-dates.ts` holds the three helpers: the day an instant falls on in a zone; an instant formatted on a zone's clock, which renders in UTC and says so for a zone it does not know; and a `YYYY-MM-DD` labelled as the day it names.
