### Fixed

- **One short or empty answer from Instagram no longer retires a carousel slide.** When a story's selected slide is missing from the children Instagram lists at preparation, the fetch now reads the post and its children a second time before judging it. The slide is retired as gone only when both reads are complete, list the same slides, and hold at least one slide and fewer than the slide count the index recorded, and the post itself still answers. An empty list, a post with no recorded slide count, reads that disagree, or reads that list as many slides as the index recorded are a change to revalidate, never a removal; a slide found by the second read is downloaded. The fetch also follows Instagram's children pages, up to the 20-slide carousel limit, instead of reading only the first. Each extra read is one of the turn's Instagram requests and counts against its budget and in its counters; the preview still reads one page.
