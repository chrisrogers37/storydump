### Fixed

- **A story parked for review before it was ever served now gets its Telegram review card, not only the text notice.** A past-posts story is served its card only once its media has passed the checks, so a refusal at its slot parks it from `scheduled` before any card exists, and the park restated only the cards a chat already had: the bound chats heard the notice and had nothing to resolve the story with. The park now also writes the story's review card, beside the notice, in every bound chat that holds no card of it; a card retired or failed before it was sent counts as none. The sender sends it as it sends every card, without a keyboard, and the edit that follows brings the review keyboard (It posted, Not there — post again, Give up, and Configure). A chat that already has the story's card gets that card restated, as before, and no second one.
