### Security

- **A card goes to a private chat only while the person whose chat it is belongs to the workspace.** A Telegram binding to a private chat (`telegram_dm`) receives a card only while the linked Telegram identity with that chat's id is a member of the binding's workspace. Every statement that queues or claims a card checks this at use time (`bindings.deliverable_binding_where`, which narrows `push_binding_where`; the sender sweep's door keeps the latter and reads no membership). An outcome still supersedes a card in every live binding, and queues its edit only where a card may go. A sender minted for a private chat whose person does not belong claims nothing and retires that chat's queue, so a card queued earlier never goes out later. Unlinking Telegram therefore stops a workspace's cards to that account's private chat until the person links again, and the Unlink confirmation says so. Groups are unaffected. No migration.
