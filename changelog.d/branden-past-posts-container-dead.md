### Changed

- **A story whose media Instagram would not process says why on its review card.** When Instagram ends a story's media container (ERROR or EXPIRED) and the retries are spent, the review card's line gains Instagram's status and its own words for it. The publish pipeline records them as a `container_dead` refusal in the story's `last_error -> 'error' -> 'refusal'`, the record a parked story's card already reads. Every restate of a story in review now carries its refusal's line, as a card rendered again already did.
- **A parked past post with no refusal evidence says why it was not admitted.** A story parked because its chosen slide or post is no longer indexed, what was chosen changed, or its media was judged one stories do not take says so in its notice, rather than that no reason was recorded.
- **A refusal's sentence names each item of a list once, and says how many more a long list holds.** A codec a file carries twice is named once.
- **The Instagram publish adapter's text passes the shared provider-text scrub.** Meta's error fields, a dead container's status and its log line, and a failed call's own text go through `refusal_evidence.scrub_text`, so the scrub now covers a token parameter whatever value it holds and keeps a signed provider link as its host alone. The scrub also masks a delivery signature outside a link, and drops NUL before it matches anything.
