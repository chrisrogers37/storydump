### Fixed

- **A Telegram approval card whose send lost its answer can no longer leave a second card with live buttons, and a card's edit is retried until Telegram confirms it (#38).** A card is now sent without a keyboard, and settling its send queues the edit that gives it the story's keyboard, so buttons only ever reach a message whose id the ledger holds. A resend after an unknown outcome can therefore leave at most one extra copy without buttons, which no tap can reach: Telegram has no idempotent send and no way to read a chat back, so that copy cannot be found or removed. Edits of a card were failed after three resends of a lost answer and never revisited, which could leave a decided story's card with its buttons; an edit is now resent until Telegram answers it (a 2xx, or "message is not modified"), for up to an hour (`EDIT_RESEND_HORIZON_SECONDS`, after which the story's next change or a tap on the card edits it again), waiting `AMBIGUOUS_RESOLVE_AFTER_SECONDS` longer after each lost answer, and the outbox sender sweep (`fn_sender_sweep`, migration `004_sender_sweep_backoff.sql`) mints the sender on the same schedule. Edits of cards are claimed before new sends, so a card's buttons follow it on the next tick. "Message to edit not found" fails an edit after one call. A tap on a decided story's card edits that message again when its newest edit did not land, and a card retired mid-send keeps its message's ref, so later lines reach it. Each card costs one more Telegram call (its send, then the edit that brings its keyboard). Invitation cards still leave with their own keyboard.
