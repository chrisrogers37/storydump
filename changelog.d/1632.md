### Changed

- **The central gate reads under its caller's tenant and never sets one (#1632).** `tenant_resolution.authorize_member` loses its `tenant_bound` flag along with the fallback that bound the claimed workspace itself, and `commands.execute` and `commands.ingest` lose the flag they forwarded. Every production caller already bound the workspace it gates before calling, so one place now binds the tenant and the gate only checks. A caller that forgets to bind is refused (`not_a_member`, under the tenant policy) rather than having its transaction bound, or re-bound, for it; a new gate case run as `svc_ingress` pins that refusal against a control that binds first. No decision changes: the membership read still binds both the workspace and the user, and the unit table now holds every role at every floor to that one read.
