### Fixed

- **A shared link shows its preview card again (follow-up to #1555).** Inlining the stylesheet put about 96 KB of CSS ahead of the page's `<title>` and its `og:` and `twitter:` tags, because React writes every hoisted `<title>` and `<meta>` after the stylesheets. The home page's `og:image` sat at byte 98,909, and iMessage showed a blank card with only the title. The stylesheet is a `<link>` again (`experimental.inlineCss` is off), so the tags sit in the first few KB, and `link-preview-head-contract.test.ts` holds it. This gives back the first-paint gain #1555 measured (home LCP 2.0 → 1.2 s under Slow 4G); getting it back without burying the tags needs a much smaller stylesheet for the public pages.
