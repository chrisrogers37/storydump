### Fixed

- **A sync read no longer retires a past post's slide on its own.** A complete read of a carousel that lacked a slide used to retire it at once (tombstone, item removed, asset deleted), for good on an old post; a read that listed no slides retired every slide no story held; and nothing was logged. Now a read that lists no slides proves nothing, and the index keeps what it holds. A complete read that lacks a slide no live story holds takes it out of the draw (its item `missing`) and keeps it; the next complete read that lacks it too retires it, and a read that lists it again brings it back. A slide excluded as unsupported is never drawn, so it still retires on one read. A slide a live story holds is never retired by a sync read, a full carousel's and a first-slide source's included: it stays for the story's own fetch to judge. Each post whose slides the sync keeps, holds out or retires logs one line naming them, and a post retired on a terminal answer to its parent read logs how many slides it removed.
