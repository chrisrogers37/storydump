### Fixed

- **A sync read no longer retires a past post's slide on its own.** A complete read of a carousel that lacked a slide used to retire it at once (tombstone, item removed, asset deleted), for good on an old post; a read that listed no slides took the whole post out of the draw and retired every slide no story held; and nothing was logged. Now a read that lists no slides proves nothing: the post lands as one not yet read, stays available, and keeps the slides the index holds. A complete read that lacks a slide no live story holds takes it out of the draw (its item `missing`) and keeps it; the next complete read that lacks it too retires it, and a read that lists it again brings it back. A slide excluded as unsupported is never drawn, and a single post's file is the post itself, so a complete read that lacks either still retires it at once. A slide a live story holds is never retired by a sync read, a full carousel's and a first-slide source's included: it stays for the story's own fetch to judge. Once a sync turn commits, it logs one line for each post whose slides it kept, held out or retired, naming them, and one for each post retired on a terminal answer to its parent read, with how many slides it removed. A story or schedule refused because its item is missing now says the item's file is no longer listed by its source, where it named a Drive folder whatever the source.
