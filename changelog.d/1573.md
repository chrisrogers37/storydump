### Fixed

- **A publish job that used up its retries could leave its story stuck in `publishing`, and its account unable to post (#1557).** When a publish job spends its retry budget, the worker parks its story for review and restates its card. The flip to review ran inside the exhausted notice's savepoint, so a card or notice that could not be written rolled the flip back with it. The job ended `failed`, the story stayed `publishing`, and it kept holding its account's publish slot (`uq_publish_exclusive`): every later story on that account found the slot busy, and no alert said why. The flip now commits in the job's own finalize transaction, ahead of the savepoint, and only the card and the notice ride it.
